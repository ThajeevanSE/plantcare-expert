# PlantCare Expert rule catalogue

Rules are educational interpretations of the sources. Accessed 28 September 2026.

## R01 Insufficient watering

IF Is the plant wilting or drooping? = Yes AND How does the potting mix feel? = Dry below the surface

THEN Water according to this plant's needs and allow excess water to drain. Check moisture before watering again.

Sources: S1, S2; sections: Watering guidance; Plant wilting

## R02 Overwatering stress

IF How does the potting mix feel? = Persistently wet AND (Are leaves turning yellow? = Yes OR Is the plant wilting or drooping? = Yes)

THEN Review watering frequency. Check drainage and, if practical, inspect the roots before adding more water.

Sources: S2, S3; sections: Plant wilting; Cultural/Environmental Problems

## R03 Standing water

IF Does the pot remain standing in collected water? = Yes

THEN Empty the saucer or outer pot after drainage. Do not leave the plant standing in water.

Sources: S1; sections: Bottom watering

## R04 Mineral salt buildup

IF Are there crusty mineral deposits on the mix or pot? = Yes AND Are the leaf tips brown? = Yes

THEN Check drainage first. Flush accumulated salts only if water can drain freely; otherwise repot in suitable fresh mix.

Sources: S1; sections: Watering to minimize soluble salt buildup

## R05 Softened water risk

IF Do you use chemically softened water? = Yes

THEN Use a suitable water source that has not been chemically softened to reduce mineral accumulation.

Sources: S1; sections: Watering to minimize soluble salt buildup

## R06 Insufficient light

IF Is new growth unusually thin and stretched? = Yes AND Is the plant in a dim position? = Yes

THEN Move gradually toward light levels suitable for the species; avoid a sudden change to strong sun.

Sources: S2; sections: Spindly growth; Bleached or whitened leaves

## R07 Sunburn

IF Do leaves have pale or bleached patches? = Yes AND Was the plant recently moved into stronger sunlight? = Yes

THEN Reduce excessive exposure and acclimatise the plant gradually to stronger light.

Sources: S2; sections: Bleached or whitened leaves

## R08 Cold injury

IF Are leaves or shoots turning black? = Yes AND Was the plant recently exposed to unusually cold conditions? = Yes

THEN Move the plant away from cold exposure and maintain temperatures suitable for its species.

Sources: S2; sections: Leaf or shoot blackening

## R09 Grey mould

IF Is fuzzy grey growth present on old leaves or flowers? = Yes

THEN Remove affected ageing leaves or flowers and improve air circulation.

Sources: S2; sections: Fuzzy gray growth on leaves or flowers

## R10 Root rot

IF Is the plant wilting or drooping? = Yes AND If inspected, are roots both dark and soft? = Yes

THEN Assess root damage. If healthy portions remain, remove rotten roots and repot in fresh suitable mix; seek help for extensive damage.

Sources: S3; sections: Root Rot & Stem Rot

## R11 Powdery mildew

IF Is white powdery growth spreading across leaf surfaces? = Yes

THEN Improve ventilation and remove severely affected leaves.

Sources: S3; sections: Powdery Mildew

## R12 Fungal leaf spot

IF Are there brown spots on the leaves? = Yes AND (Do those spots contain target-like rings? = Yes OR Are tiny black dots visible inside dead leaf spots? = Yes)

THEN Remove affected material, improve airflow and avoid splashing water on foliage.

Sources: S3; sections: Fungal Leaf Spots

## R13 Bacterial leaf spot

IF Do leaf spots look water-soaked? = Yes AND Does sticky liquid ooze from those spots? = Yes

THEN Remove affected material, avoid crowding and do not splash water onto foliage.

Sources: S3; sections: Bacterial Leaf Spots

## R14 Aphids

IF Are small pear-shaped insects clustered on new growth? = Yes

THEN Inspect closely. Physically remove small infestations or rinse a plant sturdy enough to tolerate it.

Sources: S4; sections: Aphids; Non-Chemical Control

## R15 Mealybugs

IF Are cottony white insects in leaf joints or underneath leaves? = Yes

THEN Inspect leaf joints and undersides; physically remove individual insects and recheck the plant.

Sources: S4; sections: Mealybugs; Non-Chemical Control

## R16 Spider mites

IF Are there many tiny pale speckles on leaves? = Yes AND Is delicate webbing present on the plant? = Yes

THEN Inspect leaf undersides. Rinse suitable sturdy plants to dislodge mites and monitor for recurrence.

Sources: S4; sections: Spider Mites

## R17 Scale insects

IF Are attached shell-like bumps present on stems or leaves? = Yes

THEN Inspect the bumps closely. Physically remove small infestations without damaging plant tissue.

Sources: S5; sections: Scale insects: Appearance, Detection and Management

## R18 Whiteflies

IF Do tiny white insects fly up when the plant is disturbed? = Yes

THEN Inspect leaf undersides and confirm the pest before choosing a control method.

Sources: S2, S5; sections: Leaf yellowing; Whiteflies; Managing common pests

## R19 Fungus gnats

IF Are tiny dark flies moving near the soil surface? = Yes AND How does the potting mix feel? = Persistently wet

THEN Review watering and drainage. Allow drying appropriate to the species between waterings.

Sources: S2, S5; sections: Flying insects; Water plants properly

## R20 Separate the affected plant

IF A pest rule (R14 to R19) has matched

THEN Keep this plant separate from other houseplants while the suspected infestation is investigated and controlled.

Sources: S4; sections: Non-Chemical Control
